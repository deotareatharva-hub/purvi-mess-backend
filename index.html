Our Story

Founded in the heart of Arvi, Wardha, Purvi Mess Services is the project of Narendra Deotare & Bharti Deotare.

Seeing students and young professionals struggling with fast food and unhygienic alternatives, we decided to open our home kitchen. Our mission is simple: provide wholesome, nutritious, and authentic home-cooked meals that remind you of your mother's kitchen.

Every spice is ground by hand, every roti is made fresh on the tawa, and every meal is packed with the same love we give our own children.
